This page drills the HAL A330 Student Oral Exam Learning Objectives Rev 7 (04/06/23), a training-reference document three years old. The scope, what the examiner walks and asks, is authoritative. The values in it are not: the FCOM, QRH and FOM are the primary study documents and win every conflict, every time.

Numeric claims are being verified against current manuals progressively. A green badge means checked current with its source; NOT YET VERIFIED means treat the number as suspect until the manual check lands; CONFLICT means a current manual already shows a different figure.

Walk progress ticks are in-memory only and reset on reload, by design. Nothing is stored on this device.
