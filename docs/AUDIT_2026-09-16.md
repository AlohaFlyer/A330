## 4. Quiz clarity

Method: `audit/quiz_clarity.py` over every bank (`fom_q/*.json`, `data/oe.json`, `data/limitations_drill.json`, `data/triggers.json`, `data/hot_seat_scenarios.json`, `data/jeopardy_pool.json`, `data/limit_or_bust_*.json`, `data/systems.json`, `data/mcdu.json`). Rules: question 25 words or fewer and one question mark; answer 20 words or fewer; a yes/no question is answered Yes or No first; no hedges (generally, typically, usually, etc.); no other-fleet clause in the answer; at most one parenthesis in the answer. Every flagged item was rewritten by hand or by a reviewed script (`audit/rewrite_ch*.py`), then re-scored. Originals kept in `audit/fom_q_backup/`.

Result, VERIFIED:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Items flagged | 367 | 29 |
| Long answers | 331 | 18 |
| Long questions | 80 | 0 |
| Two questions in one | 3 | 0 |
| Hedges | 5 | 0 |
| Other-fleet clause in answer | 23 | 2 |
| Yes/no not answered first | 11 | 4 |
| Two or more parentheses | 49 | 11 |

The 29 remaining are exempt on purpose:

- `data/limitations_drill.json` (14): limits with several conditions (fuel temperature by tank, ice protection, oxygen). Cutting the conditions would make the limit wrong. lim-afs-33 and lim-wght-04/05 mention the freighter variant by design.
- `data/triggers.json` (11): callout scripts. The answer is the full callout sequence, which is the point of the drill.
- `data/oe.json` (4): oe-006, oe-104, oe-129, oe-130 are unanswered workbook questions carried as such. A subagent filled them in during the pass; those answers were reverted because the workbook has no answer to quote.

Rewrite rules applied to fom_q and oe: one ask per question; bare-fact answer; yes/no first; numbers keep units and conditions; secondary facts that were in the answer moved to `src.note` (fom_q) or stay in `detail` (oe) rather than deleted. Where an agent trimmed a secondary fact outright it was a 737/787 clause (oe-028, oe-207, oe-278, oe-286, oe-328 listed above).
