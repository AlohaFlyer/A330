## Tally

Of the 16 NEEDS FOM CHECK items: **12 MATCHES, 1 STALE, 3 NOT FOUND** (not in FOM 125.1 — regulatory/AC teaching values, not adjudicated). Oddities: 1 STALE (Q43B), 1 NOT FOUND (L15 195 min), 1 MATCHES (Q24/50 nm).

**STALE items (relearn):**
