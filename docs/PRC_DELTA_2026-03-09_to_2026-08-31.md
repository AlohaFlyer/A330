## 3. What an A330 FO must relearn from this PRC issue

1. Engine-out cruise speed on the card is now 300 kt / M0.78 for both ETOPS and standard strategy. The FOM still
   says .82M/290 KIAS. Until the fleet reconciles it, know both numbers and which document Dispatch planned with.
2. Non-radar engine failure: keep transmitting on 121.5 with 123.45 as backup and consider squawking 7700.
3. The position report format, ARINC frequencies and per-FIR log-on rules moved to the new ORCN / ETOPS and FIR / OCA
   cards; the Oceanic References card is now only the RNP 4 / RNP 10 equipment list.
4. Cold weather: taxi-in time check for ice shedding, Probe/Window Heat Auto confirmation after start, and no
   spoilers / trim / flaps / After Start checklist until fully deiced.
5. RCAM card exists; RWYCC 2 whenever moderate-or-greater rain on a smooth runway or heavy rain on grooved is likely.
6. RNP AR may now be flown with the decelerated speed strategy; do not enter Vapp at the FDP if early-stabilized is
   used; no speed or altitude constraint edits once FINAL APP is engaged or inside the FDP. (Not quotable yet.)
7. Every card changed page. Old p6/p7/p8/p9 abnormals are now p1 to p4; approach profiles are p49 to p57.
